Welcome to Your Local Nature Escape

Fun Zoo is a historic, family-friendly sanctuary dedicated to bringing people closer to the wonders of wildlife. Located right in the heart of the community, we prioritize accessible paths, educational spaces, and hands on encounters designed for nature lovers of all ages.

Plan Your Visit Today

We are open all year round, offering standard, structured hours to accommodate family schedules, school field trips, and weekend outings.

  • Operating Hours: Tuesday through Sunday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Closed Mondays).
  • General Admission: Adults $5, Children $2, Toddlers under 2 years are completely free.
